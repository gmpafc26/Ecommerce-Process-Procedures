🌐 BigCommerce

Managing the Port Store website. Use BigCommerce for visibility, channels, categories, orders and customers. Product content (names, descriptions, images, prices) is managed in Lightspeed and syncs across automatically.

⇄️
Lightspeed syncs one way into BigCommerce
Product names, descriptions, images and prices set in Lightspeed will overwrite BigCommerce on each sync. Only use BigCommerce to manage visibility, channels and categories. Changes to product content made directly in BC will be lost on the next sync.
📍 How to get to products
BC Admin → Products → View
Use the search bar to find by name or SKU. Click the product name to open the editor.
1
Find the product
Go to Products → View. Use the search bar to find by name or SKU. Click the product name (blue link) to open the editor.
2
Set storefront visibility & publish to channels
New products from Lightspeed may not be visible by default. Both settings must be correct for customers to see the product.
👁️ Enable storefront visibility
Scroll to Storefront in the product editor. Tick Visible on storefront. If unticked, customers cannot see the product regardless of channel settings.
Product editor → Storefront → tick Visible on storefront
🔗 Publish to channels
Find the Channels section in the product editor. Make sure the product is enabled on all relevant storefronts — typically the Port Store channel.
Product editor → Channels → tick all relevant storefronts
💡 A product can be Enabled in status but still invisible if storefront visibility is off or it isn’t published to a channel. Both must be correct.
⚠️ If a Lightspeed sync runs after you make visibility changes, check these settings haven’t been reset.
3
Save your changes
Scroll to the bottom and click Save. Changes go live immediately.
⚠️ Product edits go live immediately on save. Double-check before saving.

Categories are what BigCommerce uses in the navigation to group products. The dropdowns and main links in the navigation bar are all categories. Here’s how to create and manage them.

📁 Creating & editing categories
1
Navigate to Categories
From the BC Admin home page, go to the Products dropdown in the left sidebar and select Categories.
BC Admin → Products → Categories
The Categories page — existing categories listed on the left
2
Create a new category or sub-category
To create a top-level category, click Create a Category. To create a sub-category under an existing one, click the three dots to the right of the parent category and select Create Sub Category.
The new category panel — only the name is required
💡 You only need to enter the category name and click Save & Edit. This takes you to another page — just click Save again and the category is created.
3
Assign products to the new category
There are two ways to add products to a category — from the individual product page, or by selecting multiple products at once from the product list.
📄 Single product
Open the product editor, scroll to the Categories section, tick the category and save. Multiple categories can be selected.
Product editor → Categories → tick → Save
✅ Multiple products at once
Select products using the checkboxes on the right of the product list. Click the three dots next to the Actions button and choose Add to Categories. Categories are listed under the first sales channel — Port Store.
Products → View → tick products → … → Add to Categories
Selecting multiple products and using the three dots menu to add to categories
💡 If a product isn’t showing on a collection page, check the correct category is ticked and the product is saved.
👤 Making new categories visible to all customers
⚠️ Because Port Store has member-exclusive products, new categories are not automatically visible to all customers. You must update customer group access for each new category you create.
1
Navigate to Customer Groups
Go to Customers → Customer Groups in the BC Admin sidebar.
BC Admin → Customers → Customer Groups
Customer Groups — update Normal and Not Current Member one at a time
2
Edit the “Normal” customer group
Click on the Normal customer group to open it. In the Group Access section, click the search icon. This pulls up a list of all categories — find and select the new category to add it to the group’s access.
Search for the new category in Group Access and select it
💡 Save the customer group once you’ve added the category.
3
Repeat for “Not Current Member”
Go back to Customer Groups and repeat the same process for the Not Current Member group. Both groups need to be updated separately for the category to be visible to all non-member customers.

📋 Lightspeed

Lightspeed is the source of truth for all product content. Changes made here sync automatically into BigCommerce. Always start here when creating or updating products.

⇄️
Lightspeed syncs one way into BigCommerce — not back
Product names, descriptions, images and prices set here will overwrite BigCommerce on each sync. Changes made directly in BigCommerce to these fields will be lost. Always make product content changes here first.

Lightspeed manages all core product data. Any changes made here sync automatically to BigCommerce.

Product name
Shown on storefront and search. Keep consistent across variants.
Description
Rich text description. Syncs to BC — don’t edit in BC.
Images
Upload here — they sync through to BC automatically.
Price & sale price
Retail and sale pricing. Numbers only, no $ symbol.
SKU / barcode
Don’t change SKU without checking inventory impact.
Brand & tags
Used for filtering in Back Office and synced to BC.

Once a product is ready in Lightspeed, follow these steps to make it live on the Port Store website.

1
Complete the product in Lightspeed
Ensure name, description, images, price and categories are all set. The product should be in Active status.
2
Trigger a sync
Lightspeed syncs to BigCommerce automatically when stock is updated. To force a manual sync, adjust the inventory quantity to the same number (e.g. set 5 → 5) to trigger an update without changing stock.
3
Check the product in BigCommerce
Open BC Admin → Products and confirm the name, description, price and images have come through correctly.
4
Set visibility and channels in BigCommerce
New products may not be visible by default. See BigCommerce → Products for the visibility and channel steps.

Troubleshooting
⚠️ Rows being skipped with no error
Usually a column mapping issue or wrong import type. Check headers match the template exactly and confirm you chose Add new products.
🔢 Barcodes show as scientific notation (8.05E+12)
The barcode column is formatted as a number. Select the column, format as Text, re-paste barcodes as values, then re-export the CSV.
📄 Duplicate products instead of variants
A variant row has a slightly different product name — even a trailing space causes Lightspeed to create a new product. Use Excel find-replace to standardise.
📁 Category not found
Category name doesn’t exactly match Lightspeed. Pull the exact name from Products → Categories in Back Office.
💰 Prices show as $0.00 after import
Price columns contain a $ symbol or comma. Remove with find-replace, re-export, then re-import using Update existing products.

💬 Customer Service Procedures
AWDS Fulfilment · Online Orders · Internal Reference
📍
Address Issues
Flagged by AWDS when a delivery address can’t be resolved
ℹ️
AWDS will contact you directly when they can’t deliver to an address - typically the suburb, city or postcode was entered incorrectly at checkout.
1
Google the address
Take the street number and name from the order and search Google Maps to confirm the correct suburb and postcode. Resolves most cases without contacting the customer.
2
If you can’t confirm - email the customer
Ask the customer to confirm their full delivery address including suburb and postcode.
📧 Suggested email
Hi [Name], We’re getting in touch regarding your recent Port Store order. Our fulfilment team has encountered an issue with the delivery address we have on file. Could you please confirm your full delivery address - including street, suburb and postcode - so we can get your order on its way? Thanks, Port Store Team
3
Update in BigCommerce and notify AWDS
Once confirmed, update the shipping address on the order in BigCommerce and pass the corrected details back to AWDS so they can proceed with dispatch.
✈️

⚠️
Expected behaviour - not a system error. The customer receives a dispatch confirmation but without tracking. Must be sourced manually from AWDS.

Reach out to Steve & Rebecca at AWDS with the order number

When AWDS can’t fulfil an item - process differs based on order size
💡
Always check before adjusting inventory. If the system shows 2+ units in stock but AWDS is showing zero, ask AWDS to do a physical stock check before making any Lightspeed changes.

Assess which item is unavailable

Missing Orders & Postal Issues
When a customer says their order hasn’t arrived
1
Check tracking in BigCommerce before responding
Look up the order and review the tracking status. In many cases the parcel has been delivered to the customer’s local post office and they haven’t been notified.
💡
A “delivered” scan at a nearby post office is the most common cause. Let the customer know to check their local post office with ID before escalating.
2
If delivered but not received - escalate to AWDS
AWDS hold the Australia Post account and must manage all postal investigations on our behalf.
🚫
Do not contact Australia Post directly - AWDS must manage all postal enquiries and investigations on our behalf.

Returns
Ecommerce returns managed via AWDS warehouse
ℹ️
AWDS manage all ecommerce returns at the warehouse and will email you with a list of items received. They also log returns directly into the shared register.
1
AWDS receive the return & email you a list
When returned parcels arrive at the AWDS warehouse, they email you with a list of items received and log them in the shared returns register. AWDS automatically return the stock to their system at this point.
📊 View Returns Register
2
Refund the order in BigCommerce & change status to Refunded
Go through the list of returns from AWDS and for each one: process the refund in BigCommerce and update the order status to Refunded.
⚠️
Stock won’t be available to customers until you complete this step. Even though AWDS have returned the item to their system, it won’t appear as available stock in Lightspeed until the return is processed on our side in BigCommerce.
